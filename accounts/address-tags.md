# Address Labels and Public Tags

Public tags and labels are explorer metadata shown to all visitors where configured. They can identify a known service, contract role or operator-reviewed entity. They do not establish legal identity, private-key control or investment quality.

Inspect the full address and source of the tag. Administrator approval and source-code verification are different workflows. A public label may change without any blockchain transaction.

For personal organization use [Private Tags](private-tags.md). For ownership challenges see [Address Verification](address-verification.md).

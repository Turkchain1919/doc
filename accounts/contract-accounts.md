# Contract Accounts

A contract address contains EVM bytecode and executes according to that code. It does not have a private key that can be recovered through TurkScan®.

A contract page can expose bytecode, verified source, ABI, constructor arguments and detected proxy implementations. Read/write tabs depend on available ABI and supported methods. Operator certification is separate from source verification.

Review upgrade authority and ownership roles. See [Proxy Contracts](../smart-contracts/proxy-contracts.md) and [Contract Ownership](../smart-contracts/contract-ownership.md).

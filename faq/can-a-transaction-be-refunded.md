# Can a Transaction Be Refunded?

A successful native transfer cannot be undone by editing an explorer page. The recipient or the relevant contract's supported logic would need to initiate any return. A failed receipt can still consume gas.

## What to check

Verify the recipient and transaction status. Contact a known recipient through independently verified channels when appropriate. Do not give a recovery phrase or pay a supposed explorer recovery agent.

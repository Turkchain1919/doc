# Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the native currency?** TURK, with 18 decimals, on chain `1919` (`0x77f`).

**Do I need a wallet to use TurkScan?** Public browsing and the tested public read APIs do not require connecting a wallet. Writes and ownership proofs have separate requirements.

**Does verified source mean safe?** No. It establishes a bytecode relationship, not a complete audit. Administrator certification is a separate label.

**Why is a price missing?** The pricing source may be unavailable. Null is not zero.

**Does a successful approval complete a swap or bridge?** No. Follow the application transaction and, for bridges, destination delivery.

**Where are API examples?** In the [API Reference](../api-documentation/introduction.md), including captured responses and explicit unavailable routes.

# Funds Sent to a Contract

A contract can only release assets according to its code and authorized operations. Some contracts can receive native currency or tokens without providing a withdrawal path.

## What to check

Find the exact contract, inspect verified source and identify whether a legitimate recovery method exists. The explorer cannot invent such a method or supply the contract's private key. Avoid repeated deposits while investigating.

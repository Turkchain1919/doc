# Private Keys and Wallet Recovery

Wallet control comes from cryptographic keys or a contract wallet's rules. TurkScan® does not store your recovery phrase and cannot reset an externally controlled account's key.

## What to check

Use your wallet vendor's documented recovery process and your own secure backup. Never enter a phrase into support forms or send it to someone claiming to verify your address. An explorer account password is different from a wallet key.

## Further guidance

[Read the detailed TurkScan guide](../security/user-safety-best-practices.md). If the issue persists, retain the public identifiers and exact error while keeping secrets out of the report.

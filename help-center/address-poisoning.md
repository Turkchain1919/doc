# Address Poisoning and Lookalike Accounts

An attacker can create activity involving a similar-looking address or a misleading token. A shortened identifier and a familiar public tag are not enough to identify a recipient.

## What to check

Copy the full address from a trusted source, compare it carefully and confirm the network. Do not copy recipients from unsolicited transfer history. Use personal labels for organization without treating them as ownership proof.

## Further guidance

[Read the detailed TurkScan guide](https://doc.turkscan.com/accounts/address-tags). If the issue persists, retain the public identifiers and exact error while keeping secrets out of the report.

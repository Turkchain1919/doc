# Connecting a Wallet Safely

Connecting a wallet can disclose your public account and network to a site. It is separate from signing a message, granting an allowance or broadcasting a transaction.

## What to check

Read each wallet prompt. Confirm chain 1919, address and requested method. Reject unexplained signatures and transactions. Disconnecting a site does not revoke existing on-chain approvals.

## Further guidance

[Read the detailed TurkScan guide](https://doc.turkscan.com/getting-started/wallet-configuration). If the issue persists, retain the public identifiers and exact error while keeping secrets out of the report.

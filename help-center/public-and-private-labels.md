# Public and Private Labels

Public labels can be visible to every visitor; private tags are tied to an explorer account. Neither changes blockchain ownership. Administrator review and an address signature are different forms of evidence.

## What to check

Choose the correct label type, use the full identifier and avoid storing secrets in notes. If a label is wrong, report the exact address and factual evidence through a known operator channel.

## Further guidance

[Read the detailed TurkScan guide](https://doc.turkscan.com/accounts/private-tags). If the issue persists, retain the public identifiers and exact error while keeping secrets out of the report.

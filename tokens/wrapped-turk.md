# Wrapped TURK

Wrapped TURK is a contract representation of native TURK intended for token interfaces. The wrapper contract, redemption rules and backing must be verified before use. A ticker such as WTURK alone cannot establish a canonical wrapper.

No canonical wrapper address or redemption test is established by this audit. Therefore this page does not supply a deposit address or promise that an arbitrary WTURK contract is redeemable.

For a proposed wrapper, verify deployed source and bytecode, deposit/withdraw behavior, upgrade authority, custody model and matching on-chain backing. Bridged representations additionally depend on their route and security model.
